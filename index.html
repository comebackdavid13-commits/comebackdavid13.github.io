FlyX Strats Guide

From download to your first clean flystrat. Do the parts in order and do not skip the practice.

1. Setup

You only do the first two steps once.

  1. Download the loader

    Download loader.exe from the link in your ticket and save it in its own folder.

  2. Allow the loader in your antivirus

    The loader uses KeyAuth for license checks, and some antivirus programs flag KeyAuth. Add the loader folder as an exclusion in your antivirus settings.

  3. Open the loader

    Start loader.exe.

  4. Enter your key

    Type or paste your license key and press Enter.

  5. Wait

    After a short moment the program opens.

Never share your key. Sharing your license gets you blacklisted without a warning.

2. Program settings

  1. Import the settings

    Use the import option in the program and load the settings file you received.

  2. Set your keybind

    Pick the key you want to use. In this guide it is called your keybind.

  3. Choose your connection

    Select Wi-Fi or Ethernet, depending on what your PC uses.

  4. Minimize the program

    Do not close it. Minimize it and start the game.

3. Practice

Learn it in two exercises. Move on only when you can do the current one reliably.

Exercise 1: the basics

  1. Build up a little

    Build a short tower, just high enough to jump off and fall for a moment.

  2. Jump and press the keybind

    Jump off, then press Alt + your keybind.

  3. Climb while falling

    While flying down, climb the build.

  4. Press the keybind again

    When you push yourself off the build while climbing, press your keybind once more. Repeat until it feels consistent.

Exercise 2: full height

  1. Build to maximum height

    Build up as high as the map allows. Make sure there is no building below you.

  2. Do the same routine

    Repeat the movement from Exercise 1, with one change.

  3. Spam F11 instead

    When you push yourself up, do not press Alt + keybind. Spam F11 quickly so the game freezes.

  4. Wait for the window to settle

    The game switches between windowed and windowed fullscreen, growing and shrinking. Wait until it stops.

  5. Press your keybind

    As soon as the window stops changing size, press your keybind.

Troubleshooting

The loader gets deleted or will not open

Check that the loader folder is in your antivirus exclusions, then download the loader again.

My key is not accepted

Copy it again and check for extra spaces at the start or end.

It does not work in game

Make sure the program is still running in the background and your connection type is set correctly.
